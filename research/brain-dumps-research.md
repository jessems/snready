# Research: Brain Dumps Problem

## Search Queries Used
1. "servicenow CSA practice questions free 2024 2025"

## Top Competitors (Brain Dump Sites)

### Competitor 1: ExamTopics
**URL:** examtopics.com/exams/servicenow/csa/view/
- **Content:** 408 questions with crowd-voted answers
- **Model:** Free with ads, premium for full access
- **Problem:** Answers debated in comments ("should be B not D")
- **Quality:** Low - no explanations, crowd-sourced verification

### Competitor 2: ITExams
- **Content:** "Actual exam questions"
- **Model:** Freemium
- **Problem:** No source attribution, potentially leaked content
- **Quality:** Unknown accuracy

### Competitor 3: SkillcertPro
- **Content:** Practice tests with explanations
- **Model:** Paid
- **Reviews mention:** "Some answers incorrect, please update"
- **Quality:** Better than ExamTopics but still issues

### Competitor 4: Marks4sure, p2pexams, etc.
- **Content:** Exam dumps
- **Model:** Paid downloads
- **Problem:** Clearly selling leaked exam content
- **Quality:** Unknown, ethically problematic

## Gap Analysis
- NO ONE is calling out the brain dump problem
- All competitors either ARE brain dumps or ignore them
- Huge opportunity for trust-building content
